# NOVOCIB

> NOVOCIB is a French life-science company (Boulogne-sur-Mer, France) providing HPLC-based analytical services and enzymatic assay kits/reagents for nucleotide and nucleic acid research, quality control, and drug discovery. Core customers: pharmaceutical R&D, aquaculture/animal feed, food & seafood quality control, and academic research labs.

Key areas of expertise: nucleotide and nucleic acid quantification (HPLC-UV), cellular nucleotide profiling for drug mode-of-action studies, seafood/meat freshness testing (K-value, ATP degradation products), recombinant/purified enzymes for purine and pyrimidine metabolism research, and ready-to-use non-radioactive biochemical assay kits (PRECICE® line).

## Analytical Services (HPLC)
- [Nucleotide & Nucleic Acids Analysis Service](https://www.novocib.com/analytical-services/nucleotide-analysis-service): Enzymatic hydrolysis coupled to ion-paired HPLC-UV for quantification of nucleotides, nucleosides, bases, RNA and DNA in microbial biomass, single-cell protein (SCP), food, and feed.
- [Cellular Nucleotides Analysis](https://www.novocib.com/analytical-services/cellular-nucleotides-analysis): HPLC profiling of 31+ cellular (deoxy-)ribonucleotides (mono-, di-, triphosphate) for drug mode-of-action, IMPDH/RNR inhibition, and antifolate studies.
- [Yeast Extract Nucleotide Analysis](https://www.novocib.com/analytical-services/yeast-extract-nucleotide-analysis): HPLC quantification of nucleosides, nucleotides, and nucleic acids in yeast extracts and single-cell protein.
- [Analytical Services overview](https://www.novocib.com/analytical-services): All HPLC-based nucleotide/nucleic acid analysis services.
